Privacy Policy for Oryn

Effective date: April 18, 2026

Oryn respects your privacy. This app is designed to help users track fasting and related wellness habits while keeping their data private and under their control.

1. Information Oryn Collects

Oryn does not collect personal data on external servers.

Any information you enter into the app, such as fasting settings, fasting history, meditation timer usage, preferences, or other app-related data, is stored locally on your device only.

2. Local Data Storage

Oryn stores app data locally on your device in order to provide its core functionality, such as:

  • Saving your fasting timer state
  • Saving your fasting preferences
  • Saving your progress and history
  • Saving your notification preferences
  • Saving your meditation timer settings

This data is not transmitted to the developer and is not shared with third parties by the app itself.

3. Feedback by Email

Oryn includes a “Send Feedback” option that opens your email app so you can contact the developer voluntarily.

If you choose to send an email, the information you include in that message, such as your email address, message content, or any details you choose to share, will be processed only for the purpose of responding to your feedback or support request.

Sending feedback is entirely optional.

4. Data Sharing

Oryn does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

Because app data is stored locally on your device, the developer does not have access to your fasting records, meditation activity, or personal wellness data through the app.

5. Third-Party Services

Oryn does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or remote data collection services for user data in its current version.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since Oryn stores data locally on your device only, you remain in control of your information.

You can delete your app data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app’s storage in your Android device settings, or
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

Oryn does not create online user accounts and does not retain personal data on remote servers.

7. Security

Oryn is designed to minimize privacy risks by keeping user data on the device whenever possible. However, no device or software environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

Oryn is not intended for children under the age of 16. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect improvements or changes in the app. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or about Oryn, you can contact:

Email: support.oryn@proton.me
